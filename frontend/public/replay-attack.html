Why Replay Attacks Threaten Smart Grids
A replay attack breaks data integrity without breaking encryption. The attacker records a valid low-usage packet (e.g. 1.4 kW at 2 AM) and re-sends it during peak load (5.8 kW at 7 PM), so the DISCOM bills far less than was consumed.

Defense: timestamps alone fail with clock drift or replays inside the latency window. Strictly increasing sequence numbers, freshness timestamps and an HMAC signature make every packet valid once and only once.
